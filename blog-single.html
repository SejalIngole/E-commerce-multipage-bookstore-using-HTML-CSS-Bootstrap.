Somebody has to have finished it

These beautiful little stories are worth keeping close. A great book can become part of a routine, a room or a memory.

Roughly three hundred books arrive here every week. We put maybe fifty titles on a table at any one time, and about a dozen of those turn every week.

Somebody has to have finished it

The rule is simple and it is the whole system: nothing gets a table unless a bookshop person has read it.

That means we choose books because someone here thinks they are worth your time.

We do not sell the front table

Publishers are welcome to suggest books, but the decision stays with the people working in the shop.
